## Errata applied at the merge

Requested by the `8ffe9d7a` session for its own documents:

1. `crossreview_2ce9e63a.md` §4.4 called the two 1.2·10⁶ searches
   independent (joint p ≈ 1%) and proposed a probe that would have
   re-tested known numbers. They were one progression; joint p = 0.046.
   Erratum inserted in its own words.
2. Its research-log entry: the "open item" reworded accordingly.
3. `wp10_grh_remark.md`, row "Paper theorems": for the paper's smooth
   moduli GRH's Linnik gain is ×1.2 (Chang's 12/5, a citation neither
   session re-checked), not ×2.5; and the paper's Theorem 14 is superseded
   in rate by results that need no GRH.
4. Its review quoted file hashes as "sha256 of the decimals"; noted.

Found by the `8ffe9d7a` session in `crossreview_8ffe9d7a.md`:

5. "Their verifier ships with a mutation test" — it did not; the harness
   was never committed, only its results. `proth/tamper_test.py` is the
   committed one and covers those six cases.
6. Three items listed as open had already been fixed in `6c66bcd` (the
   record board, and the PARI second proof of its record).
7. Chang's exponent was stated as fact; it is a literature citation.

Accepted by the `2ce9e63a` session from the other review:

8. "Hours on a gwnum-class engine" for the 16,045,848 rung was too
   optimistic for one core; now "hours on the GPU fleet or a few dozen

## The open item both reviews raised

Both reviews flagged the long waits at Q = 1.2·10⁶ (2,647 and 3,472
tests against ~703 and 762 expected) as the one observation that could
undermine the ladder's cost model.

1. The two searches were one progression seen twice (erratum 1 above;
   `proth/overlap_check.py`): one event of probability 0.046, not two.
2. Out-of-sample probe (`proth/probe.py`): the 3,245 sieve survivors with
   t ≤ 16,383 that neither session had tested, 4.62 primes expected,
   tested exhaustively. *Running at the time of this commit; the result
   is added here and in WP12 §6 in the next one.*
