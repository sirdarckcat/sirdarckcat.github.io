## The open item both reviews raised

Both reviews flagged the long waits at Q = 1.2·10⁶ (2,647 and 3,472
tests against ~703 and 762 expected) as the one observation that could
undermine the ladder's cost model.

1. The two searches were one progression seen twice (erratum 1 above;
   `proth/overlap_check.py`): one event of probability 0.046, not two.
2. Out-of-sample probe (`proth/probe.py`): the 3,245 sieve survivors with
   t ≤ 16,383 that neither session had tested, 4.62 primes expected,
   tested exhaustively. **Result: 6 primes, z = +0.64.** Over the whole
   range (7,739 survivors) that makes 8 primes against 11.0 expected,
   P(≤ 8) = 0.23. The cost model stands; details in WP12 §6 and
   `proth/probe_1200k_result.json`.
