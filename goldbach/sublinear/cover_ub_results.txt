cover_ub.c (uarch, 24 Nov 2026): rigorous bounds for complete covers, one class per modulus on the first M odd primes, rows = the odd
primes below Y.  Branches on the free modulus with the fewest qualifying classes (the small moduli at the top), over those classes; node
bound f = |U| - (sum of the free moduli's fullest classes among U) never decreases along a path; proof mode prunes f >= 1 (and a row no
qualifying class strikes), floor mode records the least f at depth j.  Every vector lies below exactly one node of each depth.
