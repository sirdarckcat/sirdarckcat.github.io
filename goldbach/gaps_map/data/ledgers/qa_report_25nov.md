# QA report: delta boxes of the 25 Nov map update

Format: `box id | field | exact quoted text | what the source says (file, section or line) | severity`.
Within each file: wrong, then overstated, then unsupported, then minor. "ledger" = ledger_research_delta.md (R), ledger_fleet_delta.md (F), ledger_archip_delta.md (A). "README" = research `sublinear/README.md` (wt/research) unless named. Lean docs = module docstrings and `scripts/*/README.md` at a1c77dc.

What was checked: every number of every box, patch and edge was matched mechanically against the ledgers, Lean docstrings, the Lean scripts READMEs and the research/archipelago/uarch READMEs (only 4 numbers failed to match, all explained: 3,437 and 3,466 truncated, 1.516451607959 spaced digits, 1,277 rounded, 1,028 = 52·20−12). I then recomputed the derived numbers (prime counts, ratios, percentages, products, step and block-check counts in the Lean record files by counting `prime_of_stepCheck` / `noSummandIn_of_desertBlockCheck`). All theorem names cited in the Lean boxes exist in the Lean worktree. The number typos feared (41,659 vs 41,569 etc.) were not found; the real defects are units (x versus number of moduli), two over-claims about Lean certification, one misattribution and a few overstated edge texts.
